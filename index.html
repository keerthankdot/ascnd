Examples of systems

Operations overhaul

Your team uses 6 tools that don't talk to each other. Projects slip through cracks. Nobody has visibility into what's happening across departments. We rebuild the entire operational flow: one connected system that tracks work, flags delays, and reports progress automatically.

Sales and delivery pipeline

Lead comes in, gets qualified, proposal goes out, project gets delivered, invoice gets sent. Right now that's 4 tools and 12 manual steps. We collapse it into one flow where each stage triggers the next automatically.

Resource planning system

You don't know who's overloaded until they burn out. We build a system that tracks capacity across your team in real time, flags imbalances before they become problems, and recommends how to redistribute work.

Client reporting infrastructure

Every client gets a different report in a different format at a different time. We build one system that pulls data from all your sources, generates branded reports per client, and delivers them on schedule without anyone touching a spreadsheet.

Full AI integration

Your business runs on decisions. We embed AI into the decision points: which leads to prioritize, which projects are at risk, which clients need attention, what to charge. The system doesn't just track your business. It thinks with you.
